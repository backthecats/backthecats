Coaching Staff & Program Grants

Coaches Wishlist Portal

Dedicated to equipping Wildcat teams with the tools, technology, and training aids needed to succeed. Submit formal program requests directly to the Booster Club Executive Board for regular review.

Submission Timeline

Requests are reviewed and approved at the beginning of the school year. For additonal requests, please submit request form and notify a member of the board.

What We Fund

Booster grants are designed to partner with our sports programs. We fund supplemental practice gear, safety aids, and facility improvements after district athletic budgets and team-generated fundraising (such as concession shifts and team events) have been applied.

Questions

If you have any questions, reach out to the board directly at nbathleticboosterclub@gmail.com.
