Our Mission

Dedicated to empowering New Bloomfield student-athletes by fulfilling coach-requested needs, elevating the game-day experience, and rallying community support for every high school program.

Fund (Coach Requests)

Purchasing essential equipment, practice aids, safety gear, and supplemental tools requested directly by coaching staff that fall outside standard school budgets.

Submit Wishlist ↓

Elevate (Wildcat Experience)

Enhancing program culture through running the spirit store, tournament hospitality rooms, senior recognition, and game-day atmospheres.

Unite (Community Pride)

Bringing parents, alumni, and local business sponsors together to raise vital funds, volunteer, and pack the stands at every event.
