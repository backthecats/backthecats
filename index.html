Our Three Pillars

Dedicated to empowering New Bloomfield student-athletes by fulfilling coach-requested needs, elevating the game-day experience, and rallying community support for every program.

Fund (Coach Requests)

Partnering with Wildcat teams to fund essential practice gear, safety aids, and facility upgrades after district athletic budgets and team-generated fundraising (such as concession shifts and team events) have been applied.

Elevate (Wildcat Spirit)

Enhancing program culture through running the spirit store, tournament hospitality rooms, senior recognition, and game-day atmospheres.

Unite (Community Pride)

Bringing parents, alumni, and local business sponsors together to raise vital funds, volunteer, and pack the stands at every event.
