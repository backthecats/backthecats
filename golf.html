What Your Entry Covers

At $125 per golfer ($500 per 4-some), the Rod Haley Memorial Scramble provides a full day of premier golf, meals, drinks, and prize opportunities.

Drinks All Day

Complimentary drinks on the course and in the clubhouse all day long.

Lunch & Dinner

Full meal service provided for both morning and afternoon flights.

Flight Prizes

Prizes awarded to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place teams across each competitive flight.

Mulligans & 50/50

Mulligans included, plus our popular 50/50 cash raffle and morning door prizes.

Schedule of Flights

Morning Flight (Shotgun Start)

Tee Off: 8:00 AM • Max 15 Teams

Check-in opens prior to tee-off. Includes complimentary drinks, riding carts, lunch following the round, and automatic entry into an exclusive door prize drawing for all morning teams!

Afternoon Flight (Shotgun Start)

Tee Off: 12:30 PM • Max 30 Teams

Afternoon shotgun start with all-day drinks on course, followed by post-round dinner and awards ceremonies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding team entry, flight prizes, or sponsorships?

Entry is $125 per individual golfer or $500 per 4-person team. Cost covers 18 holes of golf with riding carts, flighted scramble competition with prizes for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place teams in each flight, mulligans, entry into Closest-to-the-Pin and Longest Drive contests, lunch, dinner, and drinks all day long! Plus, all morning teams are automatically entered into an exclusive door prize drawing.

We offer multiple tee box sponsorship tiers open now, plus a new commemorative pin flag tier coming soon:
  • Par Sponsor ($100): Custom tee box sign + tournament website directory listing.
  • Birdie Sponsor ($125): Custom tee box sign + individual website feature & social media spotlight.
  • Eagle Sponsor ($250): Exclusive tee box sign (only sign on that tee box) + tee box tent spot + website backlink, social media spotlight, & awards ceremony callout.
  • Albatross Sponsor (Coming Soon): Exclusive custom pin flag on 1 of 18 tournament greens (yours to keep after the event) + website backlink, social media spotlight, & awards ceremony callout.

The scramble is held at Meadow Lake Acres Country Club with two shotgun starts: 8:00 AM (Morning Flight — capped at 15 teams) and 12:30 PM (Afternoon Flight — capped at 30 teams).

You can email the Booster Club at NBAthleticboosterclub@gmail.com, or contact a member of the Booster Club Presidency.

To become a sponsor, select any open tier above to open the online sponsorship form. To register a team, expand the team registration form above once the countdown timer officially opens entry.
